# Domain Glossary - Sanitized Portfolio Stub

## Purpose

Show that the agent system used a controlled glossary to avoid confusing client language with product/system language.

## Pattern

| Client Term | Internal Anchor | Rule | Confidence |
|-------------|-----------------|------|------------|
| `[CLIENT_TERM_1]` | `[DOMAIN_CONCEPT_1]` | `[REDACTED_MAPPING_RULE]` | partial |
| `[CLIENT_TERM_2]` | `[DOMAIN_CONCEPT_2]` | `[REDACTED_MAPPING_RULE]` | confirmed |
| `[CLIENT_TERM_3]` | `[DOMAIN_CONCEPT_3]` | `[REDACTED_MAPPING_RULE]` | not_confirmed |

## Safety Rules

- Keep client vocabulary separate from product vocabulary.
- Never infer configuration from industry terminology.
- Ask for evidence when a mapping affects TO BE, setup, scope, or external communication.

## Redacted

Specific logistics terms, product entities, operational sequences, and partner/system references were replaced with placeholders.
